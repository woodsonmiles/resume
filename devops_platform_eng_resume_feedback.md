## 🛠️ Section-by-Section Targeted Improvements

### 1. Formatting & Architecture
* **Add a Professional Summary:** Because your background spans Management, Cyber, and Software Engineering, a 3-sentence summary at the top will instantly position you for "Senior/Lead Platform Engineer" or "DevOps Architect" roles. 
* **Create a Dedicated "Clearance" Visual Anchor:** Do not hide your TS/SCI at the bottom. Move your clearance status to the very top header next to your name and contact info so corporate recruiters spot it within 2 seconds.

### 2. Experience Bullet Refinements

#### **Kellogg, Brown, & Root (SRE Manager)**
* *Current:* "Managed internal development platform adopted by 51 engineers..."
* *Fix:* Emphasize the platform metrics. **"Product-managed an Internal Developer Platform (IDP) that scaled to 51+ active internal engineers, reducing developer onboarding time and standardizing GitLab/Harbor configuration across 3-cluster K8s environments."**
* *Current:* "...built a Go CLI that accelerated deployments three-fold."
* *Fix:* Highlight the software engineering side of platform engineering. **"Architected and developed a custom Go-based CLI tool to abstract cloud-native complexities, accelerating pipeline execution speed by 300% across the enterprise."**

#### **Lockheed Martin (DevSecOps Engineer)**
* *Current:* "Automated OpenShift deployment pipeline..."
* *Fix:* Focus on self-service and gitops. **"Designed and automated self-service GitOps deployment pipelines via OpenShift, transitioning a single-person manual release bottleneck into an automated pipeline for the entire team."**
* *Current:* "Refactored ~1,000 lines of stateful deployment scripts..."
* *Fix:* Frame this around configuration drift. **"Eliminated environmental configuration drift by refactoring legacy imperative deployment scripts into declarative, version-controlled Helm charts."**

### 3. Education & Certifications Alignment
* **Prioritize High-Value Certs:** Move `CISSP` and `GCLD` ahead of standard certs like Security+. 
* **Leverage the Summa Cum Laude Degree:** Your Math & Computer Science degree from Wheaton College is a massive asset for Platform roles that require algorithmic thinking (like building custom CLI tools or operators). Keep it prominent.

* **Highlight Autonomous Execution:** Remote roles demand proof that you can execute without supervision. Your experience building a company-wide pipeline catalog entirely on your own initiative speaks volumes—call out that ownership.
